## Priority interpretation

High means central to the proposed encounter and sharing journey. Medium means useful broader browsing, social or management value. These judgements have not been validated by interviews. High difficulty is a reason to investigate or provide a fallback, not automatic evidence of low user value. All 19 cards have planned screen coverage; no release assignment is final.
